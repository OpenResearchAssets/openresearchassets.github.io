# Product strategy · Open-first, quality-first

**Current decision:** Launch an editorial-quality open project; validate research usefulness before any hosted inference, subscriptions, proprietary model training or infrastructure expense.

## Hypothesis

Researchers value trustworthy scientific design judgment and editable deliverables more than another enormous repository of mixed-quality images. The library should prove this through real use, before claiming commercial demand.

## Initial experiments

1. Can researchers find reusable references and improve their own diagrams using the open gallery, templates and quality checklist?
2. Does the review skill produce specific, faithful, practical corrections across teaser / method / results / table / whole-paper cases?
3. Do independent users return, contribute quality assets, adopt checklists or recommend the project without prompting?
4. Would a subset pay for a high-finish editable kit or human-assisted refinement? Test small one-time offers only after repeated non-monetary utility is established.

## Guardrails

- Do not equate GitHub stars with customer willingness to pay.
- Do not invent Quality Model accuracy or compare model quality without controlled expert evaluation.
- Do not retain or train on private manuscripts without express permission.
- Do not bundle copyrighted paper figures into paid packs.
- Prefer static hosting and local/BYOK compute. Hosted inference is not needed for this first release.
- Do not promise a commercial product or price before validating fulfillment cost and buyer demand.

## Early success measures (targets, not forecasts)

- At least 10 external researchers try one real workflow; record qualitative friction and resulting artifacts (with permission).
- At least 3 independent examples pass a human check at actual paper-layout size.
- Gather the first round of issue reports or community contributions.
- If commercial requests arise, try a limited-scope paid asset or per-figure service; record direct costs, hours and paid conversion.

## Decision gates

**Continue open:** meaningful use but no strong payment signal. Strengthen community contribution and quality standards.

**Test product:** consistent real outcomes and specific repeated requests for time-saving delivery. Introduce low-overhead one-time offers.

**Consider SaaS:** repeatable results without extensive manual labor, evidence of recurring demand, reliable economics and privacy protections. Never start by renting GPUs just to support a speculative feature.

ReferencesOriginal assetsBetter reviewsEditorial rules

01 · Share an exceptional reference

Provide the official project or source URL, creator credits, and a specific explanation of what researchers can learn from its composition and scientific storytelling.

02 · Create an editable asset

Submit genuinely editable SVG, code, PPTX or another meaningful source format. Include a preview, authorship and license, and clearly mark conceptual sketches versus experimental results.

03 · Improve quality judgment

Contribute a case where an existing quality checklist misses a material issue, or a permission-cleared before/after example with a documented improvement. Objective errors take precedence over aesthetic opinions.

04 · Preserve trust

  • Rights first. Do not mirror third-party paper images or paid assets without permission.
  • Evidence first. Never invent outcomes to make a diagram more compelling.
  • Open remains open. Community contributions are not silently repackaged as proprietary products.
  • Revision over volume. We accept fewer assets and invest more review in each.
